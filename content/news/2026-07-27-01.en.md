The following first-authored and three co-authored papers were presented at [HCI International 2026](<https://2026.hci.international/>), held in a hybrid (on-site and online) format in Montreal, Canada.
I also served as a Parallel Session Organizer of the conference.

- Koike, K., Tomoto, T., & Akakura, T. (2026). ELMER: A Taxonomy-Based Framework for Designing Observable Error Feedback to Support Exploratory Learning of Unobservable Concepts in STEAM Education. HCI International 2026, 16706 LNCS, 80–99. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29178-3_6](<https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29178-3_6>)
- Maeda, S., Koike, K., & Tomoto, T. (2026). Encouraging Higher-Quality Code Through Cost and Productivity-Based Ranking in Visual Programming. HCI International 2026, 16706 LNCS, 117–132. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29178-3_8](<https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29178-3_8>)
- Pierret Robles, J. P., Aikawa, N., Koike, K., & Tomoto, T. (2026). Hypothesis Verification Map for Supporting Active Error Exploration in Error-based Simulation. HCI International 2026, 16706 LNCS, 146–157. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29178-3_10](<https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29178-3_10>)
- Teraoka, S., Koike, K., & Akakura, T. (2026). Enhancing Learner Reflection in Essay Writing: An Adaptive Feedback System Integrating AES-Based Visualization and LLM Explanations. HCI International 2026, 16706 LNCS, 174–187. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29178-3_12](<https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29178-3_12>)
