date: 2026-09-16
draft: false
---

As of today, thirteen third-year undergraduates from the Department of Electrical, Electronics and Information Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Kanagawa University, have been assigned to the Problem-Solving Intelligence Lab (PSI Lab / Koike Lab).
They are the lab's first members since it was established in April 2026, and we will be building the lab together from here.

The full list of members is available on the [Members page](<https://koike-lab.org/en/members/>).
