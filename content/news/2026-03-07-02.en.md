The following co-authored paper was presented at the [2026 Spring Annual Conference of the Japan Society for Educational Technology](<https://www.jset.gr.jp/>).
For this paper, the first author, Soya Teraoka, received the [Student Session Outstanding Presentation Award](<https://www.jset.gr.jp/about/student-award/>).
The venue was University of Yamanashi, Kofu Campus.

- 寺岡颯哉, 古池謙人, & 赤倉貴子. (2026). 学習者の認知負荷低減を目指したLLMフィードバックの階層的提示手法の提案. 日本教育工学会春季全国大会講演論文集, 253–254.
