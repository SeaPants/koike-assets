- Koike, K., Maeda, S., Matsui, T., & Tomoto, T. (2026). What Does the Problem Actually Ask For? Programming Exercises That Accept Diverse Correct Solutions. Workshop Proceedings of the International Conference on Computers in Education ICCE 2026, accepted.
