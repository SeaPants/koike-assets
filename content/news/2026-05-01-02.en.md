The following co-authored paper, which I worked on with Chiba Institute of Technology, ranked #1 in the [March 2026 monthly download ranking of IEICE Transactions D](<https://www.ieice.org/jpn_r/ranking/transactions/monthly/202603_d.html>) — a [Japanese-language journal covering the broad field of information and systems](<https://www.ieice.org/jpn_r/transactions/wabun/index.html>).
This paper is part of the doctoral dissertation research of co-author Shintaro Maeda, for whom I serve as an external advisor, and it is a great pleasure to see that research from the educational and learning-support domain has drawn cross-disciplinary interest.

- 前田新太郎, 古池謙人, & 東本崇仁. (2026). 「より良いコード」への試行錯誤を促すゲームタスク型仮想ロボットプログラミングの提案と授業実践. 電子情報通信学会論文誌 D, J109-D(3), 91–103. [doi:10.14923/transinfj.2025JDP7001](<https://doi.org/10.14923/transinfj.2025JDP7001>)
